Matcha Rush

Run a little Japanese tea house: whisk matcha, pour lattes, drop in the ice, watch the coffee, turn the dango and pound the mochi — before your customers lose patience.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy

What the game does

  • Six stations, six gestures Whisk in a zigzag, hold to pour, tap the ice, stop the coffee in time, flip the dango, pound the mochi to the beat.
  • 18 levels, 3 chapters From the neighbourhood tea house to the summer festival and winter in Kyoto, with a new idea in every chapter.
  • Customers with character Hurried office workers, VIPs who tip a gem, tourists who order three items at once.
  • Master mode and Endless Rush A harder version of every level once you've beaten it, and an endless shift where the pace keeps rising.
  • Upgrade your tea house Better equipment with your coins, a second station for each machine, daily missions and a daily gift.
  • Nine languages English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese — with original music.

No account needed: your progress stays on your phone. The game is free, with optional rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one) and optional purchases. Anonymous statistics help balance the levels. The details are in the privacy policy.
