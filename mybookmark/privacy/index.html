MyBookmark privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

MyBookmark (Mon marque-page in French) is an Android reading tracker published by libergot. This page explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and what you can do with it.

In short: MyBookmark collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no usage analytics and no tracking. Your books, ratings and reviews stay on your phone; I never have access to them.

1. The data you enter

The books in your library, your shelves, your progress, your reading dates, your ratings and your reviews are stored only on your phone, in the app's database. They are not sent to any server: MyBookmark doesn't have one.

2. Searching for books

When you search for a book, or for a book cover, the text you type is sent to two public search services:

ServiceWhat is sentWhat comes back
Open Library (Internet Archive) Your search terms Titles, authors, summaries and covers
Apple (iTunes Search API) Your search terms, with a language and country setting Titles, authors and covers

No personal data is attached to these requests: no name, no identifier, nothing from your library. As with any internet connection, these services see your connection's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies (Internet Archive, Apple).

Downloaded covers are cached on your phone so they display faster.

3. Images from your phone

If you pick a cover from your gallery, the image is copied into the app's storage and is not sent anywhere. MyBookmark only accesses the images you select yourself.

4. Sharing and saving images

MyBookmark can create an image of your reading progress. It is only shared when you ask:

  • Share opens Android's share sheet; you choose which app receives the image.
  • Story opens Instagram's story editor directly with the image. The app identifier passed to Instagram only authorises this share; MyBookmark sends no data about you to Meta.
  • Save puts the image in your gallery, in a “MyBookmark” album.

Once an image is shared, how it is used is up to the app or service you chose.

5. Export and import

Export creates a file containing your whole library, in the location you choose. MyBookmark does not send it anywhere. Import only reads the file you select.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: to search for books and download covers.
  • Write to storage: only on Android 9 and earlier, to save an image to the gallery. Newer versions of Android don't ask for this permission.

7. Children

MyBookmark collects no data from anyone, children included. The app contains no ads and no purchases.

8. Deleting your data

All your data is on your phone: you can delete it at any time by removing books in the app, clearing the app's data in Android settings, or uninstalling it. Since no data is kept anywhere else, there is nothing else to delete.

9. Changes

If the way MyBookmark works changes in a way that affects your data, this page will be updated and its date changed.
