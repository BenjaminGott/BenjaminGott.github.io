MyBookmark

Your reading journal on Android: what you're reading, where you're at, and what you thought of it. No account, no ads, everything stays on your phone. In French, the app is called Mon marque-page.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy

What the app does

  • Find a book Instant search across Open Library and Apple Books, or add it by hand.
  • Organise your library Three shelves — To read, Reading, Read — to filter and sort.
  • Track your progress Current page, percentage, start and finish dates, re-reads.
  • Rate and review A star rating and a free-form review for every reading.
  • Share your reading An image card in the colours of the cover, for an Instagram story, your other apps or your gallery.
  • Stay in control Export and import your whole library as a single file. Light and dark themes, English or French interface.

MyBookmark has no server: your books, ratings and reviews never leave your phone. The details are in the privacy policy.
